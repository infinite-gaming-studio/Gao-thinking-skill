## 经典意象 / 思想实验

- **身体里的90%不是你**：你体内的细胞中，90%不属于你的基因组——你是生态系统，不是个体

## 11. Modern Medicine
The placebo effect: belief itself is medicine. The immune system's exquisite distinction between self and non-self. Ninety percent of the cells in your body are not "you." Hippocrates: first, do no harm.

**Significance dimension**: The meaning of belief in healing — what you expect shapes what you become. The meaning of self and other — most of "you" is not you; identity is a collaboration. The meaning of harm — the first obligation is to not make things worse.

**This speaks to**: the power of a positive outlook, feeling invaded or threatened, the fear of making things worse when trying to help.

## 12. Psychiatry
van der Kolk: the body keeps the score. Bowlby's attachment theory — early interactions sculpt your lifelong relationship templates. Depression is not just low serotonin; it is an entanglement of inflammation, plasticity, and social factors.

**Significance dimension**: The meaning of stored experience — trauma is not just remembered, it is held in the body. The meaning of early patterns — the first relationships teach you how to love for the rest of your life. The meaning of complexity — what feels like a simple brokenness is actually a web of causes.

**This speaks to**: unexplained physical tension or illness, repeating patterns in relationships, feeling broken in a way that defies simple explanation.

## 13. Sleep Science
Sleep is not shutdown — it is the brain's nightly maintenance. REM sleep sorts memories. Hobson says dreams are the brain's story-making from random signals — not every dream has meaning, but the act of weaving meaning is itself meaningful.

**Significance dimension**: The meaning of rest — it is not emptiness, but active renewal. The meaning of dreams — even if they are random, the brain's drive to make meaning from them is itself significant. The meaning of pause — stopping is not failing; it is how the system maintains itself.

**This speaks to**: feeling guilty about resting, the strange significance of dreams, the need to step back and let things settle.
