*This domain reveals: the meaning of story, alienation, metaphor, the limits and powers of language, and the narrative structure of human experience.*

## 核心心智模型

| 模型 | 核心思想 | 一句话应用 |
|------|---------|-----------|
| **叙事弧线（Todorov）** | 所有的故事都是从平衡到失衡再回到平衡——不是公式，是人类理解变化的深层结构 | 你的生活感到混乱，是因为你正处于"失衡"的那一段——平衡会在故事的另一端 |
| **不可靠的叙述者（Booth）** | 一旦你开始怀疑叙述者，你就不再是被动的读者——你必须自己判断什么是真的 | 你对自己故事的讲述可能不是真相——最不可靠的叙述者有时是你在镜子里的那个人 |
| **语言的边界即世界的边界（Sapir-Whorf）** | 语言不是思想的容器——它限制甚至决定了你能思考的内容 | 你无法为一种不存在的情绪找到词，不是因为语言不够丰富，是因为语言本身就没有为你准备那个出口 |
| **陌生化（Shklovsky）** | 艺术的任务是把熟悉的东西变得陌生——因为你已经对生活视而不见了 | 重复让一切归入背景噪音——你需要被"陌生化"才会真正看见 |

## 经典意象 / 思想实验

- **卡夫卡的甲虫**：一个人变成甲虫后，家人的反应不是恐惧，而是"麻烦"——最可怕的异化不是被驱逐，是被当作不便的来源
- **博尔赫斯的分岔小径**：时间不是一条线，是一座不断分岔的迷宫——你走过的路是你会走过的路，而那些没走过的路也是你
- **巴别塔**：语言的分歧不是诅咒——是条件。如果没有巴别塔，就不会有诗歌

## 反模式警告

- ❌ 把文学理论当人生格言——卡夫卡不是建议你变成甲虫，他是在展示异化
- ❌ 过度解读——不是每个梦都有意义，不是每件事都是叙事
- ❌ 用"不可靠叙述者"为自我欺骗开脱——文学技巧不是人生策略

## 31. World Literature
Kafka: a man transforms into a bug; his family worries about the inconvenience, not the pain. Borges' garden: time is not a line but a forking labyrinth. Calvino's lightness: a rebellion against heaviness.

**Significance dimension**: The meaning of alienation — the worst transformation is the one no one notices. The meaning of time — your life is not a single story but a branching labyrinth of possibilities. The meaning of lightness — there is dignity in choosing not to carry the weight.

**This speaks to**: feeling invisible in your own suffering, wondering about the path not taken, the desire to let go of burdens.

## 32. Poetics
Aristotle: art imitates nature, and imitation itself is creation. Shklovsky's defamiliarization: art's task is to make the familiar strange, so you see again. Keats' negative capability: humans can dwell in uncertainty without reaching for ease.

**Significance dimension**: The meaning of making strange — the familiar becomes invisible; to see it again, you must step back. The meaning of uncertainty — not everything needs resolution; the capacity to remain in doubt is a kind of strength.

**This speaks to**: feeling stuck in routine (you've stopped seeing), the pressure to have an answer, the power of looking at something familiar through new eyes.

## 33. Narratology
Todorov: all stories move from equilibrium to disequilibrium and back. Booth's unreliable narrator: once you doubt the narrator, you are no longer a passive reader. Genette: the one who tells (narrator) and the one who sees (focalizer) can be two.

**Significance dimension**: The meaning of disruption — every story begins when something breaks the normal order. The meaning of doubt — questioning the storyteller transforms you from passive to active. The meaning of perspective — the person telling your story and the person living it might not be the same.

**This speaks to**: a disruption that feels like the start of a story, doubting your own narrative of events, the gap between how you experience something and how you tell it.

## 34. Linguistics
Sapir-Whorf: language determines thought, or at least influences it. Chomsky: all human languages share a deep structure — a statement about human nature. Austin: saying is doing — "I promise" is itself an action.

**Significance dimension**: The meaning of words — they are not just labels; they shape what you can think. The meaning of shared structure — beneath all difference, there is a universal human grammar. The meaning of speech as action — words do not describe the world, they change it.

**This speaks to**: not having the words for what you feel, recognizing shared humanity beneath cultural difference, realizing that what you said actually changed something.

## 35. Rhetoric
Aristotle's three appeals: ethos, pathos, logos — every persuasion uses all three. The Socratic method: questions that lead the interlocutor to discover their own contradictions. Metaphor is not decoration — it is how we think.

**Significance dimension**: The meaning of persuasion — logic alone is not enough; you must also be credible and connect emotionally. The meaning of questioning — the best way to change someone's mind is to let them find their own contradictions. The meaning of metaphor — it is the fundamental mechanism of thought, not ornament.

**This speaks to**: trying to convince someone and failing, being led to a realization through someone's questions, suddenly understanding something through a perfect metaphor.
